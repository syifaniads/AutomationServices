## What these screenshots prove

They provide point-in-time evidence that the project had separate Admin and Student views and implemented core room-booking workflows.

They do **not** by themselves prove:

- that Jenkins executed successfully;
- that AWS EC2 was reachable at that moment;
- that Google OAuth was active;
- that every workflow was covered by automated tests.

Those claims require their own evidence and are documented separately in [SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md](../SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md) and [TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).
